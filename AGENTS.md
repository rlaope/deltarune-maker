# Studio instructions

This is an English-language DELTARUNE fan creation studio. Keep these instructions as the canonical project policy for every agent. Read [the workflow](docs/workflow.md) before producing or publishing an example.

## Verification

Choose checks for the files you actually change. For documentation, check relative links and consistency with the release catalog. For media, follow the playback, metadata, and byte comparison gates in the workflow. Report the checks performed and any unavailable verification honestly. A concept image or finished video does not establish a runnable game or editor.

## Assets and crossovers

Use official, original game assets for DELTARUNE and for every other game represented in a crossover. Preserve the recognizable sprites, portraits, fonts, voice blips, music, and sound effects appropriate to the scene. Keep untouched source copies and record any deliberate edits separately. Verify that a voice or effect belongs to the intended character and moment before using it.

Record each asset's source game, creator or rights holder, source URL or extraction provenance, and relevant file or asset identifier. Keep those credits with the example. Credit alone does not grant redistribution rights; record available permission or usage terms and keep original asset bundles outside this public repository unless their redistribution is permitted. Label any generated supporting art as generated.

## Creative direction

- Give every proposed game idea and every delivered video a standalone English posting title.
- Build a readable setup, escalation, and payoff. Show the crossover's recognizable interaction early and give the audience time to understand it.
- Use a varied cast and character-specific behavior, dialogue, and sounds. Let the chosen premise determine the cast.
- Keep dialogue large enough for a phone and away from social platform overlays. Use portraits and the original dialogue style to identify speakers.
- Preserve the original game's visual and audio identity while adapting the interaction to the premise.

## Delivery and maintenance

Keep public documentation and example text in English. Preserve unrelated work and make the smallest change that fulfills the request. Store showcase descriptions and posters in Git and approved video files on Releases. Preserve approved media bytes when publishing or downloading; update the catalog from measured files.

Keep this policy in `AGENTS.md`. `CLAUDE.md` imports it. Do not duplicate it into tool-specific files or depend on a contributor's private directories, installed skills, or personal agent configuration.
